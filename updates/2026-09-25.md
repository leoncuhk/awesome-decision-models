# 2026-09-25 · Initial source review

[Home](../README.md) · [Evidence records](../docs/evidence.md)

This is the date of this repository's review, **not the asserted release date of every linked project**. The work reviewed public documentation and reports; no model benchmark was rerun.

## What this edition adds

A task-first map, seven core model families, scoped alternatives, a source register, five contextualized numerical records and a maintenance procedure. Development and final-test results remain separate. Source-text revisions are captured where available; unknown weight revisions are not filled in from current model IDs.

## Findings that change how results should be read

**Laya:** the specialized card explicitly discloses calibration on a training slice and conflicting inherited temperature settings. Its accuracy result remains a reported task-specific result, but its released confidence should not be described as independently calibrated. The card's Jev number was imported from a different evaluation. [Source](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya-typed-decisions).

**CLM:** the inspected DeepSWE head is a trained candidate selector, with 38 held-out tasks and a Best-of-4 budget. The reported checkpoint and held-out hashes improve traceability, but were not verified against downloaded binaries here. Selection quality must not be described as independent coding-agent capability. [Source](https://huggingface.co/Contrastive-LM/deepswe-clm-heads-8k).

**Nimble:** the human-reference comparison is useful, but project-author-run. Its report says raw run/comparison outputs are gitignored; publicly described commands and manifests are not the same as a complete archived run. A later temperature fit must not be mixed into the earlier results. [Pinned source](https://github.com/bespokelabsai/nimble/blob/62076b4f2d365b5879dafcf7f6dd072a1fe76df7/docs/PUBLIC_BENCHMARKS.md).

**GLiNER2.5-Decide:** the 1B card was accessible and cross-reports the 340M result. The separate main card and referenced dataset card were not fully inspected. The table includes JevK5, not TypeSafe Jev; this edition does not repeat a supposed head-to-head Jev victory. [Source](https://huggingface.co/fastino/GLiNER2.5-Decide-1B).

**Kev:** preserve new-source development/test and trained-source distinctions. A pinned source commit is not a weight pin; updated model cards must not silently rewrite the interpretation of an earlier snapshot. [Pinned source](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/2855ba2a55a80579176a459f78b95d03548cabb5/README.md).

## Open review work

- Obtain exact evaluated weight revisions and verify artifact hashes where permitted.
- Audit currently inaccessible dataset/model cards rather than treating missing detail as negative evidence.
- Reproduce a small common task protocol, including a conventional baseline and hardware/cost boundaries.
- Add meaningful new sources through review, not by automatically importing search results or star rankings.
