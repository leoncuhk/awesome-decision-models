## Changes that matter

- **Perplexity Decider:** extend the existing family to v1.2 27B and distilled Flash 4B. Joint questions attend to each other; independent-request packing is a different operation. [E36](../docs/evidence.md#e36) preserves the author public-suite grouping comparison, including Flash's worse joint aggregate. Training-data and request-format changes are simultaneous, so their gains are not causally isolated. Official benchmark train splits are disclosed; claimed test decontamination is not an independent audit. Temperatures were fitted in joint format. Training around 32k, a 262144 runtime ceiling and a long-log spot check are different boundaries. Latest HF commits add a discovery tag only. [27B card](https://huggingface.co/perplexity-ai/pplx-decider-v1.2), [Flash card](https://huggingface.co/perplexity-ai/pplx-decider-flash-v1.2).
- **Baseten mammoth-1 / elephant-1:** one new family, with existing LM option logits, block full-attention and CE+Brier language tuning. The [report](https://labs.baseten.co/articles/mammoth-1-and-elephant-1-frontier-decision-models) says text-only training; the [current card](https://huggingface.co/baselabs/mammoth-1) says text/image training. Preserve the conflict rather than assert image supervision. Public benchmark feedback selected data mixtures; private score items and gated training data remain unaudited. No changing leaderboard rank or score is imported.
- **Jeffan:** one new text family with an inspectable conditional whole-input reread policy, distinct from StartLux thought generation. The [engine](https://huggingface.co/tattrongvu/Jeffan-31b-1710/blob/cebfa86c2bed2b5c83b12e44de4374b20c87fd79/inference/README.md) rereads below 0.9 and replaces its first distribution. Count that extra computation and cache behavior. Serving capacity, public-suite observed lengths and simulated memory limits do not validate all contexts or physical GPUs. The pinned change to Apache-2.0 is a license change, not a weight update. Training data/overlap remain undisclosed.
- **Missing legal authority:** add the [Oct21 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29961v1) and [fixed code](https://github.com/mmikaildemir/commit-or-abstain/tree/f3d49c6de7d5943bb1981c0a55d504f1bae5fa33). [E34](../docs/evidence.md#e34) is third-party-reported relative to providers: Jev's lower wrong-pick rate comes with more false abstentions than pplx1.2. Conditional denominators differ. Constructed absent options on one public English legal task do not establish real citation reliability, eliminate training exposure or identify hosted weight snapshots. We did not reproduce the result.
- **Cost-aware ticket abstention:** add [RouteGuard's Oct22 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31657v1) and [fixed code](https://github.com/sachinkg12/RouteGuard/tree/22a2c3988281bc7b2249fa8ce985678442ff1af3). [E35](../docs/evidence.md#e35) retains the negative calibration finding: isotonic correction lowers ECE while a constant score still has chance correctness AUROC. Unequal taxonomy supervision, historical endpoints, random splits and idealized human triage constrain transfer. The external Zenodo archive could not be read; publisher recomputation claims are not independent replication.

## Review scope and limits

Baseline main was `cd07f4117e4d2b7691231ee30943473ed50a2b32`, verified through GitHub before edits. Read the canonical records, repository/curator instructions and applicable prior notes. Reviewed the Oct19 source-watch queue (39 sources, six changes, no fetch errors **on that run**), then queried Oct18–25 commits in StartLux, Strands, Drex, Intern-Decision, FluidUse, Kev, GLiNER2, CLM, Nimble and Verdict. Intern's new commits add report/community/citation links; CLM, Nimble's code repository, FluidUse and Verdict returned no commits in this window. That does not establish unchanged HF weights or absence of other projects. [D3's Oct22 history](https://huggingface.co/vllm-sr/d3/commits/main) identifies a serving-command card correction; no accuracy increase is inferred. TypeSafe primitives/confidence pages were read; their distribution-derived statistics are not new calibration evidence.

Discovery searched primary arXiv papers, official HF cards, source repositories and publisher reports for bounded classification, scoring, ranking, verification, routing and abstention. Board listings supplied discovery leads, not inclusion proof. AutoTrust, GLiDE, Nimble v4 and other new board entries need further artifact/training review; no exhaustive-coverage claim. Ordinary HF API/fixed-blob reads were intermittently inaccessible, and terminal network access was unavailable. Thus this is a semantic source review, **not** a complete successful live 46-endpoint watcher run. Failures do not imply abandonment. No credentials, paid inference, external model installation/execution or private business material were used.

Canonical JSON → bilingual generation remains unchanged. Add four resources (two core families, one reliability and one evaluation), 18 source records and E34–E36: totals **56 resources, 20 core families, 217 sources, 36 evidence records**. Seven watcher endpoints on existing approved hosts bring the list to 46; no permissions, host allowlist, workflow or schedule changes. Selection and mechanism guides distinguish changed policies and score editions. Local checks and the exact publication/CI result are reported separately; source checking is not reproduction.
